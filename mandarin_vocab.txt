谅 | liàng | to forgive | 我不能原谅他 | I cannot forgive him. 
吃 | chī | to eat | 我喜欢吃苹果 | I like to eat apples.
逻 | luó | to patrol | 他去巡逻了 | He went out to patrol.
庙 | miào | temple, shrine | 他去庙里求佛 | He went to the temple to seek blessings from Buddha.
抓 | zhuā | to grab, to catch | 狗抓了孩子的脸 | The dog scratched the child in the face.
